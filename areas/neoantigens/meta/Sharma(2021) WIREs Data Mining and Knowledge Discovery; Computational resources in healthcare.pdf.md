## Abstract

A broad review cataloguing computational resources across informatics-based fields with healthcare applications - medical, bio-, chem-, pharmaco-, immuno- and clinical informatics - grouped into drug discovery, toxicity prediction, vaccine design, disease biomarkers and Internet of Things, with emphasis on freely available functional resources.

## Summary

The widest-scope paper in the collection by some distance, and the least specific to it. Its relevance is the vaccine design and immunoinformatics section, which places the epitope prediction tools here in the context of the broader resource landscape they came from.

Useful as an index of what exists rather than as an assessment of what works.

## Key points

- Catalogues resources across six informatics subfields, restricted to freely available and functional ones.
- Vaccine design and immunoinformatics is one of five groupings, situating epitope prediction in a wider landscape.
- Covers data repositories, prediction models and standalone software rather than only web servers.
- An orientation document, not an evaluation.

## Limitations

Extremely broad scope means shallow treatment: resources are enumerated rather than benchmarked, so inclusion is not evidence of quality. Its immunoinformatics coverage is a small part of a large review and is thinner than any dedicated review in this collection. Resource catalogues age quickly as URLs and maintenance lapse; this is a 2021 snapshot.
